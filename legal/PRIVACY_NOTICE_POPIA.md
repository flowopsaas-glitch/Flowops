# FlowOps — Privacy Notice (POPIA-oriented)

**Status:** Draft template for legal review — not legal advice.  
**Operator:** FlowOps (Pty) Ltd (“**we**”, “**us**”).  
**Framework:** Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) — South Africa.

---

## 1. Who this notice is for

This notice describes how we process personal information when organisations (“**customers**”) and their **users** use the FlowOps platform (the “**Services**”). It also covers visitors to our marketing site where applicable.

---

## 2. Categories of personal information

Depending on how the Services are used, we may process:

- **Identity and contact data:** names, email addresses, phone numbers, job titles.
- **Operational and logistics data:** delivery addresses, waybill references, consignment descriptions, signatures, POD images, vehicle or driver identifiers.
- **Technical data:** IP address, device type, logs, authentication events.
- **Billing data:** billing contact, payment references (payment card data is typically handled by a payment processor, not stored by FlowOps — confirm in your deployment).

Special personal information should **not** be uploaded unless strictly necessary and lawful.

---

## 3. Purposes and lawful basis (summary)

We process personal information to:

- provide, secure, and improve the Services;
- authenticate users and enforce access controls;
- communicate about incidents, billing, and product updates;
- comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

Lawful bases under POPIA may include **contract** (performing the subscription), **legitimate interests** (security, product improvement where balanced), **legal obligation**, and **consent** where required (e.g. certain marketing). Your counsel should map bases to each processing activity.

---

## 4. Sharing and subprocessors

We may share personal information with:

- **Infrastructure and communications providers** (hosting, email delivery, monitoring);
- **Professional advisers** (lawyers, auditors) where bound by confidentiality;
- **Authorities** when required by law.

A list of material **subprocessors** should be published or provided to enterprise customers as part of your compliance pack.

---

## 5. Cross-border transfers

If personal information is transferred outside South Africa, we implement appropriate safeguards as required by POPIA (e.g. contracts, adequacy assessments). Details should be completed by counsel for your hosting regions.

---

## 6. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including statutory retention periods. Tenant data may be deleted or anonymised after account termination according to product configuration and contract.

---

## 7. Security

We implement **technical and organisational measures** appropriate to the risk (access control, encryption in transit where applicable, logging, backups). No method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

---

## 8. Data subject rights (POPIA)

Data subjects may have rights to **access**, **correction**, **deletion** (where applicable), **objection**, and **complaint** to the **Information Regulator (South Africa)**. Requests should be submitted via the contact channel published in the product. Where FlowOps processes personal information purely as **operator** on a customer’s instructions, we may refer the request to the customer.

---

## 9. Cookies and similar technologies

If the product or marketing site uses cookies, describe them in a **cookie notice** linked from the site.

---

## 10. Changes

We may update this notice. Material changes will be communicated as required by law or contract.
