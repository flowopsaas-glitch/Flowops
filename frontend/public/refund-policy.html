Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Our principle: If FlowOps didn't deliver value, you shouldn't pay for it. We aim for fairness in every refund decision.

1. Subscription Refunds

Within 14 days of first payment (new subscribers)

  • Full refund, no questions asked
  • Your Workspace and data will be deleted upon refund
  • Email billing@flowopsaas.com with your account email and reason (optional)

After 14 days

  • No automatic refunds for partial months
  • If you cancel mid-cycle, you retain access until the end of your current billing period
  • No refunds for unused time in the current billing cycle

2. Exceptions (we will refund)

  • Double charges: If you were charged twice for the same period, we'll refund the duplicate immediately
  • Platform outage: If FlowOps was unavailable for more than 72 continuous hours due to our fault, you may request a prorated refund for the affected period
  • Billing error: If you were charged after cancellation, we'll refund the incorrect charge in full

3. How to Request a Refund

  • Email: billing@flowopsaas.com
  • Include: Your account email, amount in question, reason for request
  • Response time: Within 2 business days
  • Refund processing: 5-10 business days via Paystack back to your original payment method

4. Non-Refundable

  • Setup or onboarding fees (if applicable)
  • Custom development or integration work
  • Months already consumed (after the 14-day window)

5. Free Tier

The free tier has no charges and therefore no refund obligations. If you upgrade from free to paid and are unsatisfied, the 14-day refund window applies from your first payment date.
