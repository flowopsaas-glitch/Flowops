Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

FlowOps (Pty) Ltd ("FlowOps", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") and other applicable South African legislation.

1. Who We Are

Responsible Party: FlowOps (Pty) Ltd
Registration: Republic of South Africa
Contact: privacy@flowopsaas.com
Information Officer: Kader Hassen Bootha
Address: Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of personal information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, company name, role
  • Delivery data: Sender/recipient names, addresses, phone numbers, parcel descriptions
  • Driver data: Name, phone number, vehicle details, GPS location during active deliveries
  • Financial data: COD amounts, invoice records, payment references (we do not store card numbers — payments are processed by Paystack)
  • Usage data: Login times, feature usage, device type, IP address
  • Proof of delivery: Photos, signatures, GPS coordinates, timestamps

3. Purpose of Processing

We process your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Providing and operating the FlowOps platform
  • Enabling real-time delivery tracking and notifications
  • Generating proof of delivery records
  • Processing payments and invoicing via Paystack
  • Sending transactional communications (delivery updates, account notifications)
  • Improving the platform through anonymised analytics
  • Complying with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring platform security

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal information based on:

  • Contract: Processing necessary to fulfil our service agreement with you (Section 11(1)(b) of POPIA)
  • Legitimate interest: Platform security, fraud prevention, service improvement (Section 11(1)(f))
  • Legal obligation: Tax records, financial reporting (Section 11(1)(c))
  • Consent: Marketing communications, optional features (Section 11(1)(a))

5. Who We Share Information With

We may share your personal information with:

  • Paystack: Payment processing (PCI-DSS compliant)
  • Cloudflare: Content delivery, DDoS protection, tunnel hosting
  • SendGrid/Resend: Transactional email delivery
  • Your clients: Delivery tracking links, POD confirmations (as configured by you)
  • Law enforcement: Only when required by valid legal process

We do not sell personal information to third parties. We do not share data with advertisers.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active + 12 months after closure
  • Delivery/waybill records: 5 years (South African tax and commercial law requirements)
  • Proof of delivery: 3 years from delivery date
  • Usage logs: 90 days
  • Deleted accounts: Personal data anonymised within 30 days of confirmed deletion

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

To exercise these rights, email: privacy@flowopsaas.com

8. Security Measures

  • All data transmitted via TLS 1.3 encryption
  • Database encrypted at rest
  • JWT-based authentication with short-lived access tokens
  • Role-based access control (tenant isolation)
  • Automated database backups every 6 hours
  • Infrastructure hosted on secured VPS with Cloudflare protection
  • Regular security audits and dependency updates

9. Cross-Border Transfers

Your data is primarily stored in South Africa. Where data is processed by international service providers (Cloudflare, SendGrid), we ensure they maintain adequate levels of protection as required by Section 72 of POPIA.

10. Cookies

FlowOps uses essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No third-party trackers are embedded in the platform.

11. Children

FlowOps is a business-to-business platform. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it immediately.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email to account holders at least 14 days before taking effect.
