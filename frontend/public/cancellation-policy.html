Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

No lock-in. No penalties. Cancel anytime.

FlowOps subscriptions are month-to-month. You can cancel at any time and retain access until the end of your current billing period.

1. How to Cancel

  • Option A (Self-service): Go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription in your FlowOps dashboard
  • Option B (Email): Send an email to billing@flowopsaas.com from your registered account email
  • Option C (WhatsApp): Message +27 60 659 2875 with "Cancel [your company name]"

2. What Happens When You Cancel

  • Your subscription will not renew at the next billing cycle
  • You retain full access to all features until the end of your current paid period
  • No further charges will be made to your payment method
  • Your drivers will retain access until the same date

3. Your Data After Cancellation

  • 0-30 days after expiry: Your Workspace is suspended (read-only). You can reactivate by resubscribing. All data remains intact.
  • 30-60 days: You can still request a data export. Email us and we'll provide a full export within 5 business days.
  • After 60 days: Your data is permanently deleted in accordance with our Privacy Policy. This action is irreversible.

4. Data Export

Before or after cancellation, you can request a full export of your data including:

  • All waybills and shipment records (CSV)
  • Proof of delivery images and signatures
  • Client and address book data
  • Driver records
  • Financial/COD records

Email support@flowopsaas.com to request an export.

5. Reactivation

Changed your mind? You can reactivate your Workspace at any time within 60 days of cancellation. All your data will be exactly as you left it. Simply log in and choose a plan.

6. Downgrading

If you don't want to cancel entirely, you can downgrade to a lower plan or to the free tier. Downgrade takes effect at the next billing cycle. You won't lose data, but some features may become restricted based on your new plan limits.
