Lekganyane (owner), Eric Peete, Siphesihle Dhlamini, Sally Dorasamy, Jánis Myburgh, Mashudu
Sigidane. Revision ladder 0.1 (21 Jan 2022) → 5 (30 Sep 2024 "Correct the spelling error and

**every TOC train cancellation** (the governance layer over Anx 20b's 82 reasons).

Definitions: Clearinghouse (SQM → Corridor Clearinghouse → National Clearinghouse); TEMS; TOMS.
Inputs: TEMS Cancellation Reports · TOMS Incident Reports · Corridor Clearinghouse reports.
Outputs: Verified Train Cancellations of the Previous Day · Root Cause Reasons · Record of All
Changes · Actions and Trends · **Daily Handshake Reports** (ties to Anx 28).

**Business rules (verbatim):** National Clearinghouse daily **12h00 Tue–Fri, 10h30 Mondays**;
**24-hour dispute window**; root causes updated as information arrives (SQM → Corridor →
National); **cancellations older than 48 hours not open for dispute** (matches Anx 20a's >48h
gate); Board-of-Inquiry changes retrospective only on written evidence from Transnet Security or
Risk; TOCs must send designated authorised representatives; penalties/fines per signed SLAs and
contracts.

**Process:** SQM allocation (IM Operations Manager) → disputes at SQM → Corridor Clearinghouse
(IM Execution Manager: Planning and Execution) → National Clearinghouse (IM Head of Planning and
Execution; attendees incl. TFR Operations) → unresolved: Network Operations Interface Manager
investigates **within 48 hours** → report to National Clearinghouse Chairperson → IM Customer
Liaison Officer updates TEMS/TOMS → Execution Manager lists trains for re-planning to Planning
Manager.

RSE/TE/LPR/0023 (3 Dec 2009); supersedes all previous loading instructions/concessions.

modifications without Technical Fleet Owner.
