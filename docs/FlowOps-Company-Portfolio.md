### Company Overview

FlowOps is a Pan-African logistics operations platform (SaaS) purpose-built for courier companies, fleet operators, and 3PL providers. We provide the complete digital backbone that logistics companies need to manage their entire operation — from dispatch to delivery to payment — in a single unified system.

Our platform replaces fragmented tools (spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, manual phone calls) with an intelligent, automated command centre that gives operators real-time visibility across their entire fleet.

**Live at:** app.flowop.work  
**Driver Portal:** driver.flowop.work  
**Founded:** 2026  
**HQ:** Johannesburg, South Africa  
**Markets:** South Africa, Zimbabwe (expanding Pan-African)

---

### The Problem We Solve

60% of logistics costs in South Africa sit in the last mile — yet that's where operators have the **least** visibility and control.

Most courier companies still manage operations with:
- Manual WhatsApp groups for driver coordination
- Paper waybills and Excel-based tracking
- Phone calls to answer "where's my parcel?"
- No automated client communication
- No real-time fleet visibility

**The result:** Customer churn, revenue leakage, driver inefficiency, and operational blind spots that cost businesses thousands monthly.
