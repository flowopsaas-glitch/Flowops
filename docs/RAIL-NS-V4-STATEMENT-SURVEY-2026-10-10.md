## 3. Other charges & penalties (Tables 18–22)

- **Application admin fee (Table 20):** freight **R125 000 per Train Path**; passenger (long
  distance, metropolitan, tourism) **R42 000 per Train Path** — upon application, subject to
  annual escalation, **not refundable**. On approval: extra expenses at access seeker's request
  (trolley trips, in-situ inspections, pre-feasibility studies, train testing) at cost.
- **Traction electricity:** pass-through to electric-traction users only; corridor-aggregated,
  allocated on service design per flow using electric loco GTK's as driver.
- **Invoicing (5.2.4–5.2.5):** monthly in advance; invoice by the 26th; payment due within 5 days
  of receipt; VAT where applicable; Default Interest Rate on arrears (daily, compounded monthly);
  EFT in ZAR; Guarantee financial arrangement (liquidation on failure to pay).
- **Penalties (5.2.10):** Penalty Framework under development with DoT (ERT Act 2024 based);
  transitional period = monitor/report only; no financial penalties beyond the RAA until the
  Framework is implemented; 3 months' notice; Conventional Penalties Act 1962 crossover clause.
  - **Table 21 (TRIM penalties):** deviation on contractual train path + TRIM-caused cancellations
    → replacement path or credit note (full path fee refunded); liability assessed within 20
    business days after each Contract Year.
  - **Table 22 (Access Seeker penalties, 2026/27 = cancellations):** credit if reallocated before
    the weekly Do-ability or 72h+ before departure; **full cancellation within 72h up to
    departure (non-Force-Majeure) = forfeit access fees paid in advance**.
  - **5.2.11 additional charge events (8):** yard usage exceeded, standing wagons, staged locos,
    delayed arrival/departure, path cancellation, overloading, underloading, skew loading. Yard
    formula: `charge = (Total Dwell time – Designed Dwell time in yard)`; >30 min → deemed
    cancellation + opportunity cost of the next Train Path on the ITP.

## 4. Capacity framework (Chapter 4) — how the corpus fits

- 4.3.8.4: **"The published Network Register, Annexures 33a, and Annexure 33b are the basis for
  all timetable calculations and track occupancy for the annual timetable."** (Our three primary
  data sources are the declared calculation basis.)
- 4.3.8.5: Network Register read with Capacity Statement; quarterly reviews published as addenda;
  TRIM is reviewing current designs and actual travel times ("due to a lack of sophisticated tools,
  this information will not be readily available across the entire network for this publication").
- Train path capacity to terminals/ports is capped by facility capacity.
- Timetable Period in principle = TRIM financial cycle (1 April – 31 March); Table 16 = typical
  application timelines (*"TRIM will publish the timetable of dates on its website once the
  Network Statement is gazetted"). Ad-hoc process = Annexure 32.

## 5. Performance management (6.7) — Table 23 KPI pipeline

- Annexure 43 KPIs govern ("the current version of Annexure 43 comprises of three KPIs for the
  across all ten routes" — quote kept; see E-43-01 history). **Table 23** lists further KPIs in
  development: OTD/OTA within 30-min tolerance **(SPRINT data)**, delay minutes IM/TOC causes,
  slot modification requests, IM non-compliance, TOC non-usage (cancellations, no-shows),
  IM unit costs (train control **R/track-km**), safety performance sets for TRIM and Access
  Seekers. **E-NSV4-01 (preserved):** the caption reads "(ACCESS SEEKER to be amended to Access
  Seeker)".
- Direction of travel: aggregated area/depot/route KPIs → train-ID/section granularity; Daily
  Handshake reconciled data feeds performance management via the Route Logistics Forum.
